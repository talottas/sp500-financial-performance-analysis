# S&P 500 Sector Performance & Macroeconomic Shock Analysis (2012-2016)

## 📊 Project Overview

Interactive Tableau dashboard analyzing the financial performance of S&P 500 companies from 2012 to 2016, with a particular focus on **sector resilience during the 2014-2016 commodities and oil price crash**.

The analysis examines capital allocation efficiency, profitability dynamics, and structural differences across GICS sectors, providing clear insights into how different industries respond to external macroeconomic shocks.

**🎯 Live Interactive Dashboard**  
[👉 View the interactive Tableau Public Dashboard](https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/simone.talotta/viz/SP500SectorPerformanceMacroeconomicShockAnalysis/Dashboard1)

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

- **Capital Expenditure Concentration**: Telecommunications, Energy, and Utilities accounted for the majority of CapEx, reflecting their infrastructure-heavy business models and long-term investment cycles.
- **Profitability Asymmetries**: Asset-light sectors such as Real Estate achieved significantly higher profit margins compared to capital-intensive sectors like Financials and Telecommunications.
- **Macroeconomic Shock Impact**: The 2014-2016 oil and commodity price collapse caused severe net income deterioration in the Energy and Materials sectors, highlighting their high vulnerability to external price volatility.
